Impact by Sector

Integrated Solutions That Strengthen Systems and Communities

Education

We partner with Liberia's Ministry of Education and other stakeholders to expand access to quality learning through innovative programs and the WASSCELAB platform, helping high school students prepare for examination success.

Health

We advance healthier communities by supporting public health initiatives, strengthening local capacity, and improving access to quality health information and services that build well-being and resilience.

Innovation, Science & Technology

We foster innovation through digital skills, youth-led technology initiatives, and solutions such as WASSCELAB, working with partners to prepare Liberia for a technology-driven future.

Democracy & Governance

We promote transparent and participatory governance through civic education, leadership development, and strategic partnerships with institutions such as the City of Monrovia and the Liberia Anti-Corruption Commission.

Economic & Human Capital Development

We support entrepreneurship, organisational growth, workforce readiness, leadership capacity, and skills development to strengthen inclusive economic progress.

Build Lasting Impact With CIS

We collaborate with governments, development partners, institutions, and communities to turn evidence into practical action and advance sustainable societies.
